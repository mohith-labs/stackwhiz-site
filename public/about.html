About StackWhiz

StackWhiz is an independent YouTube channel for developers who like useful open source software.

We cover open source developer tools, AI coding agents and self-hosted apps. In every video we explain what the project does, how to install it, and which paid tool it can replace, so you can decide quickly whether it is worth your time.

What you will find

  • Deep dives (8–12 minutes): full walkthroughs of a single project, such as AI gateways, agent orchestration platforms, mesh VPNs and social schedulers.
  • Shorts (under a minute): a fast look at one tool, one idea, one reason to try it.

Who it is for

Software engineers, indie hackers, DevOps and SRE folks, and anyone building with AI coding agents like Claude Code, Codex and Cursor who wants to cut subscription costs and keep control of their data.

Editorial policy

We choose projects on merit. Every video links to the project's public repository in the description. If a video is ever sponsored, it will be clearly disclosed. Visuals that are illustrations rather than real screenshots are noted in the description.
